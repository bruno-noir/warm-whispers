How to Use Your Candle 🕯️

Four small steps to a perfect burn

01

Trim

Trim the wick before lighting.

02

Light

Place the candle on a stable, heat resistant surface.

03

Enjoy

Let the fragrance fill your space.

04

Extinguish

Put out the flame safely and allow the candle to cool.

Safety note: Never leave a burning candle unattended. Keep away from children, pets and flammable objects.

Candle Care

Look after it and it will look after your evenings

✂️

Keep the wick trimmed

Trim the wick before each use.

⏰

Do not burn for too long

Avoid leaving the candle burning for extended periods.

🪞

Place it safely

Keep it away from curtains, paper and other flammable materials.

💨

Avoid drafts

Keep the candle away from fans and strong air currents.

🧊

Let it cool

Allow the candle to cool completely before moving it.
